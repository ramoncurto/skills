# Semrush bounded research

Use this reference when a remediation run needs organic competitor context, keyword gaps, or
country/language demand. Semrush is a third-party estimate; GSC remains the first-party source
for the site's own impressions, clicks, queries, pages, and indexing state.

## Authorization and account boundary

- Reuse the owner-authorized correct account/profile and existing trial without asking for repeat approval.
- When the owner reports a 14-day trial, record that as its original duration. Do not assume 14 days remain, reset its start, or treat its expiry as verified.
- Observe and record the actual subscription status, expiry, quota, and available tools before research.
- Research authorization does not authorize a new trial, renewal, upgrade, cancellation, or billing change. Do not assume API entitlement, bypass quota, or rotate accounts to evade limits.
- Do not record account emails, IDs, credentials, tokens, or secrets.
- See [Semrush billing FAQ](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1013-billing-faq) for current billing/plan semantics.

## Bounded collection

1. Prioritize product-relevant organic competitors, Keyword Gap, and country/language demand within the observed allowance; include backlink gaps only when useful and available. Do not target every competitor keyword irrespective of visitor intent.
2. The director gathers provider evidence once; Luna workers consume bounded exports and deduplicate queries.
3. Record database, country, device, date range, filters, row/export limits, report name, and observation time.
4. Do not consume scarce country/database allocations blindly; use public research and GSC when scope is not justified.
5. Save permitted, bounded exports in the project's evidence directory during the current authorized run before access expires. Schedule future work only when requested.
6. Use [Keyword Gap guidance](https://www.semrush.com/kb/28-keyword-gap) and retain the source URL/report identity.

## Interpretation and handoff

Estimates do not establish GSC performance, indexing, ranking, traffic, approval, or revenue. Keep
Semrush estimates separate from GSC observations and label unavailable, stale, or quota-limited
fields `UNKNOWN`. If access, expiry, quota, or feature limits block a report, preserve findings,
report the limitation, and continue useful GSC/public research; never buy or bypass access.

Use a compact opportunity ledger with: topic/keyword, market/language, competitor URL, own URL/gap,
intent, estimate, GSC evidence, confidence, and next action. Treat export rows as bounded samples,
deduplicate before synthesis, and preserve database/country/device scope with every conclusion.
Check representative live SERPs and page intent before proposing page changes. Research supports
prioritization; it does not prove that a full content, authority, and measurement program is complete.
