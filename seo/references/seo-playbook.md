# SEO readiness and bounded operations

Verified 2026-08-22. Source hierarchy: official search documentation, named deployment evidence,
reproducible local tests, then hypotheses and business analytics.

## Page readiness

Page readiness is a bounded checklist, not a ranking guarantee: HTTP and render behavior, robots,
canonical, sitemap and internal discovery, indexability, intent fit, non-commodity unique value,
firsthand or expert evidence, entity/author trust, authority/mentions, distribution, conversion and
retention instrumentation, and cannibalization/control-group design. CTR curves, word count, H1 count,
RSC/payload, and cache are contextual diagnostics; they are not universal requirements.

New sites require a demand, competition, authority, and distribution baseline. If the site has no
history or the external evidence is unavailable, record UNKNOWN rather than forecast a date, rank,
indexing result, traffic, revenue, or promotion.

## International and structured content

For international SEO, use separate URLs for language or locale versions. Add reciprocal
`hreflang` annotations and an `x-default`, expose the visible language on the page, and avoid forced
IP- or language-based redirects that hide alternate variants. These controls improve discoverability
and interpretation; they do not guarantee indexing or ranking.

Structured data must represent the page's visible main content, use only supported types and
feature-specific properties, and be checked with Rich Results Test and URL Inspection. Structured-data
eligibility is not a guarantee; there is no guarantee that a rich result, citation, ranking, or traffic
will appear.

Use people-first content with original value and clear Who, How, and Why signals. Do not turn a
template or automated volume strategy into scaled content abuse; assess whether the material is
substantive, useful to people, and supported by appropriate experience or expertise.

## Source versus rendered evidence

An HTTP fetch with a Googlebot user-agent is source-fetch HTML. It is not rendered Google evidence.
Use URL Inspection or an equivalent rendered test for Google-observed claims, and label each result
as official requirement, diagnostic, hypothesis, or outcome observation.
